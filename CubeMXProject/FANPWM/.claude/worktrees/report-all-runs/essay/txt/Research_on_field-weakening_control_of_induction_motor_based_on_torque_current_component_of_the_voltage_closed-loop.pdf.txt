Abstract—The paper is based on vector control research on 
field-weakening control strategy of induction motor. In field-
weakening induction motor torque output declines,in order to 
improve dynamic performance,on the basis of the existing voltage 
closed-loop field-weakening control strategy,the torque current 
tracking error is handled on the calculation of the reference 
excitation current in field-weakening region,so as to improve the 
tracking ability of current and torque of the motor output 
properties. Use MATLAB/SIMULINK simulation model of 
induction motor field-weakening control system. System 
simulation and experimental results will be proved the feasibility 
and effectiveness of the method. 
Keywords—vector control;torque current tracking error;field-
weakening;voltage closed-loop;induction motor 
I. INTRODUCTION 
Existing induction motor field-weakening control strategy 
can be summarized as follows: (1) The traditional method of 
1/Ȧr is that reference rotor flux and above base speed is 
inversely proportional to the actual speed of the rotor [1]. The 
method for a given excitation current and without considering 
the voltage influence current regulation, so it is difficult to 
achieve optimal control field weakening area. (2) Based on 
method of motor model is the excitation current of this control 
strategy based on motor model and through calculation 
analysis.This method is required to set up complex motor 
model and has strong dependence on more sensitive to motor 
parameters, so poor robustness
[2-3]. (3) Based on voltage 
closed-loop control method is that will feedback voltage and 
maximum voltage form a closed-loop, the residual quantity 
through the design of field-weakening controller into the 
required excitation current
[4-7]. The method is effective in 
reducing the dependence on motor parameters, good robustness, 
but there are strong coupling excitation current and torque 
current, current regulator easily saturated state. 
 This paper to improve the current tracking ability and the 
ability of the motor torque output, combining the current 
voltage closed-loop control strategy, this paper proposes a 
consider torque current component of the voltage closed-loop 
control strategy. By reducing the excitation current to improve 
the system dynamic characteristic, make the system tracking 
characteristic and torque output ability reached an ideal state. 
This paper is that the torque current tracking error is handled 
on the calculation of the reference excitation current in field-
weakening region. On the basis of theoretical analysis to build 
simulation model, and then on asynchronous motor driven 
platform to write software realization algorithm, simulation 
and experimental results verify the effectiveness of the 
proposed method.

III. M
ODIFIED FIELD-WEAKENING CONTROL ALGORITHM 
So-called field-weakening control is that under the 
condition of limited voltage, by reducing the magnetic field 
intensity,  ensure enough torque current in order to improve the 
speed and  torque output capacity. Field-weakening phase 
using voltage closed-loop control strategy is implemented by 
controlling the output to the motor stator voltage maximum 
torque. Current and voltage limitation determines the 
maximum output torque of the motor. Maximum torque mainly 
depends on the current limitation and magnetic flux level,so 
the reasonable distribution of current is the key to achieve 
maximum torque increase. According to the d/q axis stator 
current and torque is directly proportional to the product 
principle of reasonable allocation of excitation current and 
torque current. By the formula (3),the motor output torque is 
proportional to the excitation current component 
isd and torque 
current component isq of the product. When motor running in 
the region of the field-weakening torque output capacity 
depends on the coordination control of  isd and isq
[9]. While the 
isd  and isq is determined by  u sq and usd, the torque output 
capacity depends on the coordination control usq and usd. 
According to the voltage equation, due to the usd and usq 
coupling, the size of the current isd and isq is inevitably affected 
and voltage should follow the constraint condition at the same 
time. So we design a current regulator output using the same 
limit value of voltage 
usd and usq. According to the formula (1) 
and (2),with the increase of motor speed, q axis voltage 
component will increase rapidly which reduces the impact

voltage limiting state, feedback excitation current component 
has a poor dynamic performance and increase the induced 
voltage of the q axis component further. The output torque of 
motor performance is affected. This method is by changing the

In this paper modified field-weakening control strategy is 
simulated,comparison with the traditional voltage closed-loop 
control strategy. From three field-weakening control key data, 
two kinds of control method can satisfy the requirement of 
stability and fast response and can be used as  field-weakening 
control induction motor. From Fig. 2(a) shows that the field-
weakening control method of this paper, the time required to 
achieve the same speed is less than the traditional control 
method and the speed to follow and accelerate is superior to the 
traditional control method.From the simulation results, the 
traditional voltage closed-loop control method in torque 
10N · m, to achieve a given 300 rad/s need time for 1.02s, and 
the modified method need only 0.92s. From Fig. 2(b), the 
excitation current is realized by using the field-weakening 
control method into steady state quickly, dynamic process 
smooth and current tracking performance improvement. From 
Fig. 2(c), after entering field-weakening area torque to reduce 
gradually, the electromagnetic torque generated by the 
proposed approach is bigger than using the traditional control 
method of amplitude. In this paper torque is about 
6.5N · m ,but the traditional method is about 5.6N · m,increase 
about 20%,after the speed is constant and stable. Simulation 
results show that under the same load conditions, the modified 
voltage closed-loop field-weakening control method compared 
with traditional voltage closed-loop field-weakening control 
method, the motor speed tracking faster, smoother startup 
torque current, smaller excitation current.
